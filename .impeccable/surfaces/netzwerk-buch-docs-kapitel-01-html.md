## Direction contract

THESIS: The book is read at night on a freshly groomed slope: everything dark and calm, light only where there is something to see. Refuses the dark docs template of grey panels and a blue accent, and refuses light-on-everything.

OWN-WORLD: Night ground near black with a blue cast, snow white text, grey-blue for secondary, turquoise as the only accent for "here" and "act" (changed from orange on the user's request, 2026-11-03), red only for emergency and open points. The chapter head is a plain grey band (dark grey at night, light grey by day), clean, with no texture or picture (user request 2026-11-03, replaced corduroy and then ridges). A light day variant exists as a user toggle; dark stays the identity. Archivo (condensed to wide) for navigation and headings, Source Sans 3 for reading. Tables as lit rows on the night ground, status by shape.

STORY: The reader finds the chapter, reads a calm column, scans tables quickly, and always sees fixed, decided, estimated and open apart.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width chapter head about 150 to 200px tall as a plain grey band, book name top left, beacon dot plus the part of the book (the chapter number stays in the stand line beside it, amended 2026-11-03 to avoid saying it twice), the title large at the reading column's left edge inside the head, allowed to run into the right column so it stays on one line.
