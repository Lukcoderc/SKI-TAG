## Overview

Redesign of 2026-11-06 (Lukas: "modern, krass, but clean, Apple style"). The base stays: a bright snow page, Funnel Display and Funnel Sans, ScrollCraft acts. What changed:

- **Light and night.** Snow sections alternate with night-blue stages (`.nacht`, `#0b1226`, the brand blue of the app). The night stages carry the technical moments: what is inside the chip, how help arrives, and the closing line. Every section paints its own background, so the page never passes through a muddy grey while the engine's background drift catches up; the boundaries between snow and night are hard, as on Apple's product pages.
- **The chip is the hero object.** It is shown in signal orange (decided in the hardware workshop book for visibility in snow) with a graphite TPU sleeve and collar and a white button, never as a flat line drawing again. In the hero and in "Was drinsteckt" it is a real-time three.js model; everywhere else it is the top-view SVG symbol `#chip-oben` (240 × 120, the 2:1 ratio of 120 × 60 mm).
- **Orange is the product, red is the alarm.** Orange marks the chip, eyebrows, the group call and app actions. Red (`#ff2d2d`) stays reserved for the emergency: the LED once a call runs, the cancel ring, the SOS square in the app, the staff alert.
- **Big type, few words.** Each section opens with a small orange eyebrow and a two-line title: the first line in ink, the second either softened (`.leise`, 42% of the current colour) on snow or in the orange gradient (`.verlauf`) on night.

## The 3D chip

`site/assets/chip-modell.js` builds the model in millimetres (scaled 0.01), used only in "Was drinsteckt" since the hero went back to a drawing: Unterteil and Hülle (orange printed tray, graphite TPU sleeve), Qi coil (copper spiral), LiPo 1000 mAh with label, Lade-Modul and vibration motor, perfboard with the RAK radio module, GPS patch antenna, motion sensor and battery gauge, the orange lid with LED and an embossed "SkiTag", and the white button in a graphite collar. Order and names follow Maximilian's exploded drawing; the outer size 120 × 60 × 32 mm (prototype with sleeve) and signal orange are decided in the workshop book (chapter 2), the parts in its chapter 6 (as of 2026-11-08): radio module RAK3172 on the RAK3272S adapter, GPS Seeed L76K, motion sensor LIS3DH, battery gauge MAX17048, flat LiPo 1000 mAh with protection circuit (50 × 34 × 5 mm), Qi receiver with charging at 0.5 A, a 16 mm waterproof button (IP67), a red-green LED, a 10 mm coin vibration motor, the case printed in PETG or ASA with a TPU sleeve. Real part sizes are in its chapter 5; the inner sizes in the model are illustrative, and the page no longer says so (hint captions removed on 2026-11-07). Rendering: three.js r169 self-hosted under `site/assets/vendor/` (import map, no CDN), ACES tone mapping (other tone mappers turned the orange salmon), RoomEnvironment reflections, transparent canvas. The scene renders only while its section is on screen.

"Was drinsteckt" (`drinsteckt.js`) explodes the model in three stages with the scroll progress, the labels (open-3d-skills `explode.js`, patched for one right-hand column) fade in as their part comes loose; since 2026-11-08 they speak to customers, not engineers: what each part does for you, no sizes, capacities or module names (Knopf "kurz drücken: Gruppe · halten: Hilfe", Licht "zeigt, was der Chip gerade tut", Funk und GPS "sendet, wo du bist, ohne Handynetz", Vibration "spürbare Antwort in der Jacke", Akku "hält einen ganzen Skitag", Kabellos laden "ganz ohne Stecker", Weiche Hülle "schützt bei Stürzen"; present tense since Feedback 24 on 2026-11-08, although the workshop book still marks battery life as estimated and the drop test as open; the flat fallback drawing uses the same words and lost its dimension line), the LED glows red near the end, dragging sideways turns the model. Night lighting: a soft top light from behind, a cold blue rim, a warm side light, a dark contact shadow that follows the lowest part. Without WebGL a flat side view after the workshop drawing is shown, in the same colours.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do show the chip as the orange object, in 3D or as `#chip-oben`. Don't draw it square or as an outline again.
- Do keep red for the emergency only; orange is not a warning colour here.
- Do write product claims in the present tense, as working features (since 2026-11-08, Lukas's decision); no "Ziel", "soll", "geplant" or test notes on the page. No separate small hint captions (removed at Lukas' request, 2026-11-07): no "Platzhalter", "Modell nach …", "Beispieldaten", "Vorführung" pill or footnotes.
- Do give every section its own background colour.
- Don't name a ski area, a partner, a price or a date. Don't show the server, cables or charging stations (Lukas, 2026-11-04).
- Don't show speed or speed rankings in the app screens.
- Don't load fonts, scripts or models from a CDN.
